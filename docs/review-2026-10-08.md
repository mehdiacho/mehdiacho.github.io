## What does not yet match the latest brief

1. **Personal narrative.** The hero is first-person, but the enormous list of services, process strip, “What I’m hired to do” section and repeated sales prompts still dominate. Lead with the person and interests; put practical commission details on relevant pages. CAD is a personal practice with requests considered individually, not a claim of being a seasoned studio.
2. **Hero motion.** The role list is static. A restrained rotating word slot or type-and-delete treatment is still to be designed, with a readable static/reduced-motion state and stable layout.
3. **CAD interaction.** The public gallery only has two still images. Its optional model viewer requires `item.model`, which is not populated. The blueprint images are not the interactive HTML drawings.
4. **Software presentation.** The bento pager works, but covers retain the old black/cyan diagram style. Choose actual product screenshots where useful and purpose-specific illustrations elsewhere. Review card cropping, text density and some softer shapes while retaining the section identities.
5. **Claims.** Current visualisation copy calls Desk Twin a measured reconstruction and says it works on any room. Desk Twin's own records say dimensions remain partly estimated and no physical survey was supplied. Keep this an honestly labelled work in progress.
6. **Publishing and privacy.** Blog, newsletter, privacy/cookie UI and copyright line are not present. Analytics currently starts after page load without a consent choice. Inventory actual data collection before writing policy or adding a newsletter provider. Accounts are explicitly a later idea.
7. **Content source.** Public software cards refresh from Firestore. Editing `constants.ts` alone changes prerendered/fallback copy, not necessarily the copy visitors see after the live request. Align the two when revising project stories.
